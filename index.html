Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Anything else, just ask — no obligation and no hard sell.

Do you book flights?

Yes — flights are part of what we arrange. There's no search tool on this site, though: you tell us your routes and dates and we come back with the sensible options and a clear price.

Can you work to a fixed budget?

That's the usual starting point. Give us a number and we'll be honest about what it buys — and where spending a little more actually changes the trip.

How far ahead should I get in touch?

Two to three months is comfortable for most trips, and longer for peak dates and honeymoons. Short-notice plans are often possible too — message us and we'll check straight away.

Do you help with visas and documents?

We'll point you to the requirements for your nationality and destination, and help you line up the paperwork with your itinerary. Applications and decisions stay with you and the embassy.

How do payments work?

You'll get the full schedule in writing with your quote, before anything is confirmed. Nothing is charged without your go-ahead.

What if something changes while I'm away?

Message us. We handle rebookings, delays and supplier issues so you're not the one on hold with a call centre in a different time zone.
